Questions, answered

Before you begin.

When does Clariva use AI?

Only after you grant in-app permission and explicitly request an AI insight, follow-up question, or weekly report. Clariva does not continuously analyze while you write.

Can I set a daily reminder?

Yes. You can choose one daily reminder time in Settings, then change or turn it off whenever you want.

Can I protect the app?

Yes. App lock can use Face ID, Touch ID, Android biometrics, or your device passcode fallback. This setting stays on your device.

Which languages does Clariva support?

The app is available in English, Polish, and Ukrainian. AI reflections adapt to your selected language.

What does the trial include?

New accounts include 20 AI credits. Clariva Pro includes unlimited optional AI insights; in-app store pricing is shown before you decide to purchase.
