Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Clariva ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Who We Are

Clariva is a personal journaling and mood tracking app. For privacy questions, contact us at: taraschuiko@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data needed to provide the app experience.

2.1 Account Data

  • Email address (used for authentication)
  • Account creation date

2.2 App Content

  • Daily check-ins: mood score (1–5), energy level, sleep quality, and optional note
  • Journal entries: free-text content you write
  • AI analysis results: reflections, patterns, and suggestions generated by our AI provider
  • Weekly reports: aggregated mood trends and AI-generated narratives

2.3 App Settings

  • Language preference
  • Subscription status (trial or pro plan)
  • Usage counters (number of AI analyses used)

2.4 Technical Data

  • Firebase Authentication user ID
  • RevenueCat anonymous user ID (for subscription management)
  • App crash reports (if applicable)

3. What Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos or camera (unless you paste content into the journal)
  • Microphone or audio
  • Device advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history
  • Any data from other apps

4. How We Use Your Data

PurposeData Used

Storing your check-ins and journal entriesCheck-in data, journal text

AI analysis is only performed when you explicitly tap "Analyze this entry" or "Generate report." We never automatically process your journal content.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Firebase (Google LLC)

We use Firebase for authentication and data storage.

  • Your data is stored in Firebase Firestore under your user account.
  • Firebase encrypts data at rest and in transit.
  • Privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5.2 Google Gemini (Google LLC)

When you request an AI analysis or weekly report, the relevant journal text or aggregated check-in data is sent to Google Gemini for processing.

  • Your data is not used to train Google's AI models.
  • Google does not retain your data after returning the response.
  • Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.3 RevenueCat, Inc.

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions.

  • RevenueCat receives your app user ID and subscription event data.
  • Payment processing is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) — we never receive your payment details.
  • Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Data Retention

Your data is stored in your Firebase account for as long as your account exists.

  • You can export all your data at any time from Profile → Privacy → Export your data.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Profile → Privacy → Delete your account. Deletion removes all check-ins, journal entries, AI analyses, weekly reports, and your authentication account.

After deletion, no personally identifiable data is retained by us. Google and RevenueCat may retain transactional data according to their own policies.

7. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Firebase Authentication protects access to your account.
  • Firestore security rules ensure each user can only access their own data.
  • AI analysis and weekly report collections are read-only from the app — they can only be written by our server-side Cloud Functions.

8. Children's Privacy

Clariva is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at taraschuiko@gmail.com.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data (use the in-app export feature)
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data (use the in-app delete feature)
  • Correction: Update your account information
  • Portability: Export your data in JSON format via the in-app export

For requests that cannot be fulfilled through the app, contact us at taraschuiko@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
